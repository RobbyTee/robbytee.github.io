Our roots

Named for Drayton Mill

Drayton Mill's red-brick spinning mill was built between 1902 and 1904 just outside Spartanburg. For most of a century it kept the community running. It was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2012 and has since been reborn as the Drayton Mills Lofts.

We think that's the right way to build a website: solid foundations, honest craftsmanship, and the flexibility to adapt as your business grows. That's why our logo pairs the mill's tower with a wifi router. It's local heritage, wired for what's next.
