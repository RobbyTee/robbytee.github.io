Our story

Slow dough. Early mornings. No shortcuts.

Every loaf starts the night before. We mix, fold and wait, giving the dough the hours it needs to build flavor that no mix or machine can fake. By the time the street lights click off, the ovens have been roaring for hours.

We bake in small batches, with flour from growers we know by name and butter that would make a Parisian blush. When it's gone, it's gone. That's how you know it's fresh.

What we're known for

Three good reasons to set your alarm a little earlier

Coffee worth slowing down for

Small-lot beans, roasted for sweetness and pulled with care. Order a velvety flat white or a bright pour-over, or ask us what we're excited about this week.

Bread with backbone

Crackling baguettes, tangy sourdough and hearty seeded loaves, shaped by hand and baked dark the way bread should be. Grab one warm. We won't tell.

Pastries that flake on contact

Laminated by hand over three days. Buttery croissants, almond twice-bakes and a rotating case of seasonal treats that tend to vanish by noon.
